What We Offer

Why Join UTSA NSBE?

Academic Excellence

Access tutoring, study groups, and mentorship from experienced engineers to excel in your coursework.

Career Development

Connect with industry leaders, attend career fairs, and gain real-world experience through internships and networking events.

Community & Support

Be part of a supportive network that celebrates diversity and uplifts Black engineers across the country.

Professional Growth

Develop leadership skills and attend national conferences to expand your professional network and knowledge.

Scholarships & Awards

Access exclusive scholarship opportunities and recognition programs designed to support your academic journey.

Corporate Partnerships

Engage with leading companies and explore career opportunities with organizations committed to diversity and inclusion.
