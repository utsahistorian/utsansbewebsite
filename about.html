Our History

From humble beginnings to national impact — the journey of NSBE and our UTSA chapter through the decades.

1975

The Foundation

NSBE was founded at Purdue University by Edward Barnette and Fred Cooper to address the high dropout rate of Black engineering students.

1976

National Formation

The first national meeting was held at the University of Illinois, officially establishing the National Society of Black Engineers.

1981

Incorporation & Growth

NSBE was incorporated as a non-profit organization in Texas, enabling rapid expansion across universities nationwide.

2002

UTSA Chapter Founded

The UTSA NSBE chapter was established, bringing academic excellence and professional development to San Antonio.

2026

Modern Era

Today, NSBE boasts over 31,000 members across 250+ student chapters. Our UTSA chapter continues to grow and innovate.
